Game Book: Shooting Diary

Support

An iPhone app that keeps the record a bound game book keeps: every day in the field, the bag by species, the guns you were out with, and the season laid out as a ledger. Photograph the paper game card at the end of the day and the app reads it, so the copying is done for you.

Report a problem

Problems, questions and requests all go to the same place. You do not need to know anything technical to post one.

Open an issue

A free GitHub account is needed to post, and takes a minute to make. Everything raised there is read.

What helps when reporting

  • Which iPhone, and which version of iOS.
  • The version and build shown at the bottom of the app's Settings screen.
  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.
  • For a card the scan read badly, a photograph of the card is the single most useful thing. Cover or blank any names first if you would rather not share them; the layout is what matters.

Common questions

Where is my diary kept?

On your iPhone, and nowhere else. There is no account to make and no server holding your entries. That also means the only complete copy is the one on the phone, so take a backup (see below) and keep it somewhere safe.

How do I back it up, or move it to a new phone?

Settings, then Full Backup. That writes a single file holding every entry and every photograph, which you can save to iCloud Drive or email to yourself. Restore from Backup reads it back. Restoring only ever adds entries; it never overwrites or deletes anything already in the diary, so restoring twice does no harm.

Can I get the diary out as a spreadsheet?

Settings, then Export Spreadsheet. One row per outing, which opens in Numbers or Excel.

Why does it ask for my contacts?

Only so you can pick the people you were out with from your address book instead of typing every name. The contacts are read on the phone and are never uploaded. Decline and everything still works; you type names by hand.

Why does it ask for my location?

To record where a day took place, and to look up the weather for that place on that date. Decline and you can still set the place by hand or choose it on the map.

The app says a species is out of season. Is it stopping me?

No. It flags and it never blocks, and it is a reminder rather than legal advice. Open seasons vary by country, by species and by sex, and they change. Check the current dates with BASC or the equivalent body where you shoot.

The scan misread my card.

Nothing is filled in until you tap Apply, so a card it reads badly costs one tap to discard. Game cards vary enormously between estates and the app learns nothing about a layout it has not seen. Sending a photograph of a card it struggles with is what makes it better.

Why are game, vermin and deer kept apart?

Because a game book keeps them apart. A pigeon shot on a driven day is game; the same bird on a vermin round is not, and only the entry knows which. The three totals are never added together.
